# KriyoPay market review and revised pricing

Research date: 23 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata. Revised commercial proposal, not an implemented billing change. This replaces the earlier pricing recommendation for discussion.

## Recommendation

Offer Basic at ₹2,999/month, Pro at ₹7,999/month and Enterprise from ₹19,999/month. Make core AP controls available in Basic, include a scoped accounting connector in Pro, and sell Enterprise through integration, capacity and implementation value. Treat these as pilot prices pending unit-cost and customer acceptance validation.

There is no verified exact equivalent to KriyoPay's entire AP-plus-fleet scope. This review covers 12 relevant products across direct AP competitors, document automation and adjacent accounting/spend platforms. It is not an exhaustive list of every product. Quote-only vendors cannot be ranked by price without matching written quotes. No sales outreach or quote request was sent.

### What the evidence supports

The earlier ₹7,500/100-invoice Basic was a difficult entry offer against public organization-based pricing and bundled capture/workflow features. This is a commercial inference, not customer willingness-to-pay evidence.

Public AP starting prices span low-cost organization plans, per-user subscriptions, metered extraction and custom enterprise quotes. There is no defensible single market average across these units.

KriyoPay should be positioned as invoice-control software for teams using Tally or another accounting system. It is not a full replacement for the general ledger, GST filing, banking, card issuance or a mature procurement suite. It cannot truthfully claim to be cheaper or more capable than all competitors.

For an actual competitive bid, request the same scope from each vendor: Indian legal entity, 250/1,000/3,000 supplier invoices monthly, five-page documents, 5/15/40 internal users, PO/receipt matching, intake, vendor portal, one/two accounting connections, training, year-one total, renewal price and payment/usage charges. Do not send such requests without explicit user authorization.

### Feature details and practical boundaries

Basic: a team captures invoices, reviews extracted values, compares POs/partial receipts, routes approval, resolves supplier questions, records actual payments and exports reviewed CSV. Include supported configured email intake; current Gmail sync is bounded, read-only and not an unlimited real-time service. Vendor portal links require controlled distribution. Do not promise automatic vendor invitation emailing.

Pro: add bulk work, supported rules, note adjustments, statement reconciliation and a validated accounting connection. Read-only API keys do not imply a public write API; webhooks require operated delivery workers. Operational GST/tax flags are arithmetic/format screening, not government verification or filings.

Enterprise: baseline connector allowance applies to two supported connections in one company scope; it does not include multiple legal entities or consolidated accounts. Dynamics 365 Finance and bespoke mappings need separate discovery and professional-services quotes. Unknown remote financial writes require reconciliation. Accounting payment exports do not transfer money.

ERP acceptance must follow docs/erp-integrations.md. Customer credentials, ERP permissions, accounts, taxes and ledgers must be validated. Tally inventory PO/receipt writes are unimplemented; ERPNext outbound records are drafts; Zoho goods receipts need its Inventory companion connection; ERPNext/Zoho withholding export limitations remain. An included connection is a supported scope after acceptance, not universal two-way ERP compatibility.

### Fleet bundle

Enterprise optionally activates Fleet & Trips for 25 active vehicles and 500 new trips/month within the same company. Included scope: vehicle/driver/customer records, transport orders, trip milestones, delivery proof, reviewed trip expenses, customer invoices/collections, supported driver earnings, advance recoveries, settlement review and operational reports.

Pro customers can add the same fleet allowance for a proposed ₹2,999/month. Trip invoices count against the fleet trip allowance in this commercial design; supplier bills still count against AP invoice capacity. Separate these meters in the implementation to avoid accidental double charging. Vehicle/trip allowances are not established current enforcement.

Fleet excludes GPS hardware, live tracking, route optimization, statutory payroll and bank transfer execution. Additional vehicles/trips are quoted until load and support cost are understood.

## What to build next for competitive depth

These are roadmap priorities, not currently included delivery promises:

1. Finish production hardening: enforce branch access on every relevant read/write/export, administrative MFA, managed private storage, monitoring and tested backups/restores.
2. Prove connector quality: accountant-validated mappings, sandbox acceptance, reconciliation and visible actionable sync errors for target customer ERPs.
3. Expand approval convenience: validated reminders, delegation and escalation with explicit rules and audit history. Current availability is not assumed.
4. Add budget reservations and department/project spend controls after data ownership and permission design.
5. Introduce stronger bank reconciliation suggestions with confidence and human approval; preserve exact matching as the current limit.
6. Add verified GST/vendor checks through a properly configured provider; current tax flags are not verification.
7. Add SSO and multi-entity consolidated reporting only after implementation, testing and contract scoping.

Do not sell tax filings, statutory payroll, bank feeds, automated payouts, SOC 2/ISO certification, autonomous AI approvals or an uptime guarantee solely because competitors advertise them.

| Enterprise extra invoice allowance | ₹4,999 per 1,000 invoices for the subscription month |
| Extra internal user | ₹199/month, subject to tier/contract capacity |
| Pro Fleet & Trips | ₹2,999/month, 25 vehicles/500 trips |
| Extra ERP implementation/entities/migration | Written fixed scope and quote |
| WhatsApp intake | Provider/configuration/usage quote after acceptance; no unlimited-message promise |
| Dedicated infrastructure or 24/7 staffed support | Separate feasibility and commercial agreement |

No per-supplier directory charge. Normal master-record limits must still be published after capacity testing; this does not promise unlimited storage or records. Publish concrete storage, retention, file-size, API and webhook allowances before launch. Vendor portal identities do not consume internal-user seats; owners, finance, approvers and auditors do. Seat enforcement currently needs role-aware validation if catalog semantics change.

One distinct accepted supplier invoice consumes one unit. Failed uploads/rejected duplicates, field corrections and platform-failed retries do not consume another unit. Accepted invoices remain counted even if later deleted. Annual customers receive monthly allowances; unused invoice units do not roll over. No charge for new allowance without explicit customer purchase. Re-extractions requested by a user and documents over five pages require a disclosed rate, determined after cost measurement.

At 80%/100% capacity, warn customers and offer top-up/upgrade. Existing financial records remain accessible for authorized review/export; don't freeze a financial review midway to upsell. Implement these rules before advertising them; the current metering is not asserted to match this proposal.

## Cost guardrail for low prices

Proposed minimum recurring gross-margin target: 70%, measured on net subscription revenue, including annual discounts and all direct recurring service costs. This is a target, not an observed margin.

| Plan | Average monthly revenue under annual billing | Maximum direct monthly recurring cost at 70% margin | All-in cost ceiling per invoice at full allowance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic | ₹2,499.17 | ₹749.75 | ₹3.00 |
| Pro | ₹6,665.83 | ₹1,999.75 | ₹2.00 |
| Enterprise baseline | ₹16,665.83 | ₹4,999.75 | ₹1.67 |

Cost ceiling covers extraction, retries, hosting/database, document storage, routine support, recurring integration operations and payment-collection fees. It is not an AI-only budget. Enterprise fleet costs also consume its baseline budget. Deduct shared tenant and support costs before allocating the remainder to document processing.

Pilot with paid customers in each target volume band. Measure accepted invoices, pages, actual provider cost, failed/retried extraction, storage growth, support hours, connector incidents and fleet use. Price one-time integration labor separately. If these ceilings cannot be met, change the allowance or price before general release; do not fund unlimited manual support from document revenue.

Avoid perpetual introductory discounts or unlimited expensive AI/chat usage. Rich workflow features can be broadly included because document volume, heavy integrations and professional services provide clearer commercial boundaries.
