## Cross-cutting dependencies

- Preserve Next.js, Django/DRF, PostgreSQL, existing auth and Razorpay subscription billing.
- Branch filtering is blocked on a consistent branch relationship for operational/finance records and query-level authorization.
- GST, e-way bill, e-invoice, FASTag, vehicle, bank, fuel-card, map/routing and GPS integrations require official access and commercial/credential review. Provider interfaces do not mean a live connection exists.
- Browser GPS is foreground/session-dependent. Continuous background tracking requires dedicated hardware or a later authorized mobile product; do not claim otherwise.

## Immediate build sequence

1. Complete the LR vertical slice and verify tenant isolation, numbering, cancel audit and print behavior.
2. Add a reviewed branch-aware data/API matrix before migrating business records; migrate a narrow fleet slice with backfill and isolation tests.
3. Complete booking-to-trip-to-POD workflow and its validations.
4. Design inventory as immutable stock movements connected to procurement and maintenance.
5. Define the accounting data model and reconciliation boundaries before posting financial events into a general ledger.
