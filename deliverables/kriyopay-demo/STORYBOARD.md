# KriyoPay — 90-second product showcase

Format: 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 90 seconds, English narration and burned-in captions.

This is an animated feature overview using illustrative screens and sample data, not a live product recording. Feature groups are summarized; this is not a detailed demonstration of every interaction. External integrations require configuration. Payment records and reconciliation do not imply execution of bank transfers.

## 00–06s — MEET KRIYOPAY

Meet KriyoPay. Connect invoice processing, transport operations, driver settlements, and financial controls in one organized workspace.

Coverage: Capture & review invoices; Plan & track transport; Settle & reconcile payments; Report with accountability; Finance teams; Transport operators; Business owners.

## 06–12s — 01 / DAILY WORK

Start with your overview and attention inbox. Find invoice issues, pending approvals, overdue payments, fleet follow-ups, and outstanding salaries.

Coverage: One attention inbox; Clear ownership; Follow-ups & notifications; Invoice review; Exceptions; Approvals; Overdue payments; Fleet follow-ups.

## 12–18s — 02 / INVOICE INTAKE

Upload invoices or bring them in through email. Automatic extraction prepares editable fields, with confidence flags, duplicate review, and clear retry options.

Coverage: Upload or email intake; Automatic field extraction; Confidence flags & retry; Processing status; Duplicate review; Bulk actions; AI review.

## 18–24s — 03 / PROCUREMENT

Connect supplier invoices with purchase orders and goods receipts. Manage vendors, aliases, financial notes, and portal access while reviewing differences.

Coverage: Purchase orders; Goods received; Invoice matching; Resolve differences; Vendors; Vendor aliases; Vendor portal; Credit & debit notes.

## 24–30s — 04 / REVIEW & APPROVAL

Route exceptions, prepare follow-up drafts, and apply approval and SLA rules. People make approval decisions, with activity history for accountability.

Coverage: Flag exception; Draft follow-up; Route for approval; Record decision; Approval rules; SLA rules; Human approval; Activity history.

## 30–36s — 05 / PAYMENTS

Record payments and allocations, then reconcile imported bank transactions against supplier payments, customer collections, and driver settlements, with duplicate protection.

Coverage: Payment allocations; Duplicate protection; Confirm bank matches; CSV / XLSX import; Customer collections; Driver payments.

## 36–42s — 06 / FLEET PLANNING

Manage trucks, drivers, and customers. Turn transport orders into assigned trips, and coordinate the day from the dispatch board.

Coverage: Trucks & drivers; Customer directory; Transport orders; Create & assign trips; Dispatch board; Trip list; Dedicated feature pages.

## 42–48s — 07 / DRIVER EXPERIENCE

Drivers update trips from a phone: pickup and delivery, odometer readings, receipts, photos, and signatures. Save updates offline and sync later.

Coverage: Pickup & delivery check-ins; Odometer readings; Receipts & delivery proof; Offline save & later sync; Delivery photo; Customer signature; Assigned trips.

## 48–54s — 08 / TRIP COST CONTROL

Track fuel and trip expenses against budgets. Review mileage, profitability, and low-margin alerts to investigate unusual costs and missing records.

Coverage: Fuel & expense budgets; Mileage monitoring; Profitability alerts; Trip reports; Low-margin alerts; Missing-record follow-ups.

## 54–60s — 09 / DRIVER FINANCE

Calculate driver pay using monthly, trip, or kilometre policies. Track advances, approved deductions, payments, reversals, and balances in an exportable ledger.

Coverage: Monthly, trip & kilometre pay; Advances & recoveries; Approved deductions; Audited corrections & reversals; Evidence & reviewer approval; Salary payments; Date-range ledger export.

## 60–66s — 10 / CUSTOMER BILLING

Connect completed transport work to customer invoices. Record collections, follow outstanding balances, and carry that context into reconciliation.

Coverage: Complete trip; Prepare invoice; Record collection; Track outstanding; Customer invoices; Trip billing context; Collection reconciliation.

## 66–72s — 11 / REPORTS & CONTROLS

Review operational and financial reports. Export trip costs, salary balances, exceptions, and payment status, with tax review, Tally exports, and audit history.

Coverage: Trip costs & salary balances; Invoice exceptions & payment status; Tax review & audit history; Date-range exports; Operational reports; Tally export; Recorded user actions.

## 72–78s — 12 / AUTOMATION

Configure email intake, API access, webhooks, WhatsApp workflows, and custom fields. Choose reminder recipients, channels, and escalation rules for overdue work.

Coverage: Email intake; API & webhooks; WhatsApp workflows; Reminder rules; Custom fields; Recipients & channels; Escalations; Notifications.

## 78–84s — 13 / ADMINISTRATION

Control workspace roles, branches, settings, and billing. Internal admins manage companies, fleet access, plans, subscriptions, usage, payment records, and security.

Coverage: View, edit, approve, pay & export; Fleet feature access; Company & subscription setup; Staff roles; Payment records; Feature overrides.

## 84–90s — KRIYOPAY

KriyoPay brings finance and transport workflows together. Explore a guided walkthrough tailored to your team, your operations, and your next stage of growth.

Coverage: Invoices to reviewed records; Orders to completed trips; Earnings to settled balances; Operations to useful reports; Book a guided walkthrough; Explore your own workflow.
