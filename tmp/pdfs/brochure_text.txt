How usage works
Invoice allowance. One distinct accepted supplier invoice consumes one unit. Failed uploads, rejected duplicates and
field corrections do not consume another unit.
AI allowance. One extraction of up to five source pages is included per invoice. Platform-failed retries do not consume
another allowance. Larger documents and user-requested re-extractions need agreed pricing.
Monthly reset. Invoice packs apply to the subscription month and do not roll over. Annual plans still receive monthly
capacity.
User seats. Owners, finance users, approvers and auditors count as internal users. Vendor portal identities do not
consume internal seats.
Additional limits. Storage, retention, file-size, API and webhook allowances are specified in the final service schedule;
no unlimited-use promise is implied.
All displayed rates are proposed and exclude applicable taxes. Invoice capacity purchases are explicit. Additional
companies, third-party licenses, bespoke work and dedicated infrastructure are separately scoped.
